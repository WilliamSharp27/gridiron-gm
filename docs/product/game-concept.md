# Game Concept

## Vision

Gridiron GM is a franchise-management simulation in which the player succeeds by building a coherent football organization rather than simply collecting the highest-rated players.

The user is the general manager. The game focuses on roster construction, cap/resource allocation, coaching, scheme fit, team development, and long-term decision-making. On-field outcomes are simulated from those management decisions.

## Core Fantasy

The player should feel like the executive running a professional football franchise:

- identify roster strengths and weaknesses
- scout and draft players
- negotiate in free agency
- execute trades
- manage contracts and future roster flexibility
- hire and replace coaches
- choose organizational schemes and philosophies
- balance immediate contention against long-term sustainability
- respond to injuries, development surprises, chemistry issues, and performance swings

## What Makes the Game Distinct

### Talent is multidimensional
Players have position-specific attributes rather than one dominant overall score.

### Fit matters
A player's production depends partly on how well his attributes match the offensive or defensive scheme and the role assigned to him.

### Coaching matters
Coaches influence tactical efficiency, development, culture, adaptability, and the value extracted from the roster.

### Chemistry matters
Continuity, leadership, role satisfaction, winning, coaching stability, and personality compatibility affect team performance.

### Context matters
Home-field advantage, weather, fatigue, injuries, travel, and seasonal variance modify expected outcomes.

### Outcomes remain uncertain
A stronger team should win more often, not always. Controlled randomness creates realistic upset potential while preserving the value of good management.

## Initial Scope

The first playable version should support:

1. a fictional league and teams
2. generated players and coaches
3. roster and depth-chart management
4. draft
5. free agency
6. trades
7. contracts / roster-budget constraints
8. coaching and scheme selection
9. possession-level game simulation
10. season schedule, standings, and playoffs
11. aging, development, injuries, and offseason progression
12. save/load franchise state

## Out of Scope for the First Version

- graphical real-time football gameplay
- licensed NFL/NCAA teams, logos, player likenesses, or proprietary data
- detailed agent negotiation simulation
- multiplayer leagues
- full play-by-play tactical control

These can be reconsidered after the core GM and simulation loop is stable.
