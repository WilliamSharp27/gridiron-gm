# Fictional League Design

## Objective

Gridiron GM will use an original professional football league so game mechanics, branding, history, and data can evolve without depending on licensed NFL or NCAA intellectual property.

## Initial League Assumptions

Recommended starting structure:

- 24 to 32 teams
- two conferences
- multiple divisions per conference
- regular season
- postseason tournament
- annual amateur draft
- free agency period
- player trades
- salary / roster-budget system

The exact team count and schedule format should remain configurable rather than embedded in simulation code.

## Team Identity

Each franchise should contain:

- city / market
- original nickname
- original primary and secondary colors
- original stadium name
- conference and division
- market characteristics
- home-field characteristics
- franchise history generated or authored for game flavor

Real geographic city names may be used. Team branding should be original and should avoid names, visual identities, color treatments, stadium branding, or other combinations likely to be confused with existing professional or collegiate teams.

## Data-Driven League Configuration

League structure should ultimately live in configuration/data files rather than application code. This will allow:

- alternate league sizes
- expansion
- relocation
- custom fictional leagues
- future modding
- different schedule models

## Content Generation

During development, AI-assisted prompts can be used to propose fictional teams, players, coaches, stadiums, and history. Generated content should be reviewed for accidental resemblance to existing sports properties before inclusion in public builds.

## Competitive Balance

Initial team rosters should not all be equal. The generated league should include:

- contenders
- average teams
- rebuilding teams
- young high-upside rosters
- veteran rosters
- scheme-driven teams
- teams with cap/resource problems

This creates meaningful franchise-selection and rebuilding scenarios from the first season.
