# Coaching and Scheme Model

## Coaching Roles

Initial coaching model:

- Head Coach
- Offensive Coordinator
- Defensive Coordinator

Additional roles such as position coaches, scouting staff, or strength/conditioning can be added later.

## Head Coach Attributes

- leadership
- culture building
- player development
- game management
- adaptability
- staff quality / coordinator management
- offensive philosophy
- defensive philosophy

## Offensive Coordinator Attributes

- scheme family
- play-calling quality
- quarterback development
- offensive-line utilization
- run/pass balance tendencies
- adaptability
- situational efficiency

## Defensive Coordinator Attributes

- base front
- coverage preference
- pressure philosophy
- front-seven utilization
- secondary utilization
- adaptability
- situational efficiency

## Scheme Families

Initial offensive scheme families may include:

- West Coast / timing
- vertical passing
- spread
- Air Raid-inspired
- power run
- zone run
- play-action / multiple

Initial defensive scheme dimensions may include:

- 4-3 vs 3-4 structural preference
- one-gap vs two-gap tendency
- man vs zone coverage emphasis
- single-high vs split-safety preference
- conservative rush vs pressure-heavy approach

Schemes should be expressed as weighted preferences rather than rigid labels.

## Scheme Fit

Scheme fit should compare a player's attribute profile and archetype to the demands of the assigned role.

Example:

```text
Player Scheme Fit =
  weighted match between player attributes
  and role/scheme requirements
```

Fit should influence effective performance but not erase raw talent. Elite players should remain valuable outside their ideal scheme, while marginal players may become useful when strongly matched to a system.

## Coaching Effects

Coaching can modify:

- scheme efficiency
- player development
- chemistry and culture
- game-to-game consistency
- situational performance
- ability to adapt when opponents counter the preferred scheme

The coaching model should avoid excessive stacking. Head coach and coordinator effects should be bounded and tested independently.
